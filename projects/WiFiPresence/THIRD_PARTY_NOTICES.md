Repository: https://github.com/francescopace/espectre; tag 3.0.0, commit 12fce9354d3506290e70a853a4f22866f0e987ef. Its source declares GPL-3.0-only with a separate commercial-licensing option. The GPL text is preserved in LICENSES/ESPectre-GPL-3.0.txt. The dependency is installed separately; its source/license/headers are not relabeled as team work.
