# Review checklist

- Trace every conclusion to source, operator record, measurement, or software check.
- Keep all original files unchanged and failures visible; verify hashes.
- Confirm labels, field geometry, calibration factor, whole-trial split, and quality policy.
- Do not train on current failed S1 or diagnostic/held-out data.
- Report valid coverage/UNKNOWN, per-condition outcomes, and unavailable latency.
- Keep real measurements and generated fixtures separate.
- Update requirements/status/handoff when implementation changes; preserve negative results.
- Verify public paths, links, dependency versions, and personal-site file preservation.
- Obtain each member's own explanation; do not infer approval from names.
